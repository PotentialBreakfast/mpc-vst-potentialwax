= START
# PotentialWax: dig, flip, play
A melodic sampler. Pick a song from your own library, or let it dig one out, and it finds 16 cue points, works out the tempo and key, and matches them to your project.

- Load it as a plugin instrument (a plugin program). The pads play from the cues.
- It needs a folder of songs: put them in a folder called MELODIC LIBRARY at the top of an SD card, USB drive or SSD.
- MP3, WAV and FLAC. After adding songs, tap RESCAN on the BROWSE page.
- Tap RANDOM TRACK, or ROLL for a random song played a random way.

The full PotentialSuite manual: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/potential-plugins

= CONTROLS
# WAX page
- RANDOM TRACK digs out a song. PREV and NEXT step through them. LIBRARY switches folders. BROWSE lists 10 at a time.
- CUES ON: where the 16 cues go. Phrases (4-bar phrase starts), Bars or Hits. FIND CUES rolls a new set.
- NUDGE moves the last pad's cue.
- BEAT CHOP puts the 16 pads one beat apart: 4 bars, from the bar of the last pad you played.
- MODE: Pads (each pad plays from its cue) or Keys (the last pad, spread over the keyboard from the ROOT note).
- PAD PLAYS: Hold, To Next Cue, 1 Bar or 2 Bars.
- /2 and x2 halve or double the tempo it found, for half-time and double-time samples.
- PLAY SONG and STOP play the whole song.

= MATCH
# Tempo and key
- TEMPO: Original, or Match MPC.
- KEY: Original, or any key from C to B. It moves the shortest way. PITCH adds up to 12 semitones each way on top.
- KEY LOCK keeps one key for everything you dig. Switch it on with KEY on Original to take the key of the song you are on.
- PITCHING: Keep Tempo (time-stretch, the speed stays put) or Vinyl (speed and pitch move together, like a turntable).
- The MPC does not tell plugins the project's key, so KEY is where you set it.
- A file that is a whole number of bars (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32) is recognised as a cut loop and gets its exact tempo.
- Tempo is found between 75 and 150 BPM, or read from a name like 92bpm.

= WAVE
# WAVE page
- The song's waveform with all 16 cues. Playing a pad selects it. PAD arrows pick one without playing.
- FLIP: REVERSE plays the selected pad backwards, HALF SPEED an octave down, FILTER puts a low-pass on it. Each pad keeps its own.
- VIEW: the whole song, or 16, 4 or 1 bar around the selected cue.
- Move the selected cue by a bar, a beat, a 1/16 note or a tiny step (5 ms). PLAY PAD to hear it.
- SNAP TO HIT moves the cue onto the nearest real hit. RESET CUE puts it back where it was found.

= SAVING
# Saving your work
- Your project remembers the song, the cues and every setting.
- SAVE KIT writes an MPC drum program: each pad as a WAV, as you hear it (tempo, key, pitch and flips), on pads A01 to A16, with a MIDI file that plays the pads in order. MPC OS 3.10.1 or later.
- SAVE PADS writes the 16 chops as WAV files.
- Kits go to PotentialWax/Kits on the song's drive, pads to the PotentialWax folder there.

# Where it finds your songs
- A folder called MELODIC LIBRARY at the top of any drive.
- Any folder at the top of a drive with melodic, wax, flip or songs in its name.
- Folders you list in PotentialWax/folders.txt on that drive, one per line.

= SUITE
# With the other plugins
- Dusty Loop chain on the Wax track (soul, jazz and library loops): Bleed > Patina > Grit, the same preset name in each.
- 90s Stab chain for chopped horns, keys and vocal stabs: Grit > Punch > Bleed > Patina.
- PotentialCrates finds and plays records. Record what you like into the MPC, save it to your MELODIC LIBRARY folder, RESCAN, and flip it here.
- PotentialWarp as the last insert for half time and tape stops on the chops.

Effects go on the track's inserts. SAVE KIT saves the pads as you hear them in the plugin, before the track's effects.

= HELP
# Good to know
- If pad 1 does not play the first cue, use LEARN on the SETUP page, then hit the pad you want as pad 1.
- Only the first 6 minutes of a song are used. Files shorter than about 2 seconds are skipped.
- Tempo, key and cues are a best guess. Putting the BPM in the file name fixes the tempo.
- Keep Tempo sounds more stretched on big tempo or key changes. Vinyl has no stretch artefacts.
- 8 voices, 44.1 kHz playback. CHOKE and RELEASE are on the SETUP page.
- Q-Links: PITCH, VOLUME, TEMPO, KEY, CUES ON, PAD PLAYS, MODE and PITCHING.
- Looks: Print Plate, Night Rack or Colour Block, on the SETUP page.
